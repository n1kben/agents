# Investigating

Investigating is read-only: answer with evidence and make no changes. Cite a source for each claim.

## How

Start where it's triggered and follow each step to the part that does the work. Read the source; don't guess from names or docs. Mention anything surprising, and say what you couldn't trace.

## Why

Read its history back to where it started, including old versions, the discussions around them, and deleted docs. Say "because" only when you can quote the reason; mark the rest as guesses. Point out decisions that were later reversed.
