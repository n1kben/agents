Demo for a teammate who was not there.

Tell it as a user story. Show, don't tell.

Use the real thing with realistic data, after your last change. Rehearse, then record: no dead air.

Caption every clip with what it demonstrates, without covering what it shows.

Show what changed against what was there, and pre-empt the obvious questions.

Flag judgment calls and rough edges. End with how to try it.
