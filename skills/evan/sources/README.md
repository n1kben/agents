# Source corpus

This folder records the public evidence used to build the skill. It is a research index and synthesis, not a mirror of Evan Czaplicki’s work.

## Contents

- [synthesis.md](synthesis.md) — evidence-backed conclusions and confidence limits.
- [talks.md](talks.md) — talks, interviews, transcript availability, and thematic notes.
- [primary-works.md](primary-works.md) — papers, the Elm Guide, Elm news, and related primary writing.
- [forum-reading-notes.md](forum-reading-notes.md) — representative Discourse threads and what they reveal.
- [social-reading-notes.md](social-reading-notes.md) — short-form register evidence and access limits.
- `discourse-index.csv` — metadata and direct links for 42 topics and 277 replies visible through the public Discourse API on 2026-09-12. No post bodies.
- `elm-news-index.csv` — 35 Evan-attributed news/release pages found in the Elm website source.
- `guide-index.csv` — 43 Guide chapters with word counts and source links.
- `gists-index.csv` — metadata for 38 public gists returned by GitHub’s API.

## Method and limits

Research favored sources authored by Evan or official archives. Secondary commentary was used only for discovery or triangulation. Full copyrighted works and bulk post bodies are not reproduced; notes are paraphrases, and the indexes retain URLs for verification.

“All posts” is feasible for the visible Elm Discourse corpus as a metadata index. It is not feasible for X/Twitter: X does not expose a complete public archive here, search engines are incomplete, third-party mirrors are unstable, and deleted or protected posts may be unavailable. The social notes therefore use a clearly labeled sample rather than claiming completeness.

The corpus spans 2012–2026. Views can evolve. When answering what Evan currently believes, verify against the newest primary source rather than treating this synthesis as permanent.
