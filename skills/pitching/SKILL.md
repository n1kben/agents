---
name: pitching
description: Pitch ideas or proposed changes one at a time. Use when the user wants to evaluate options or approve changes before they are applied, or uses a "pitch" trigger phrase.
---

Pitch ideas or changes for me to evaluate. Start with pitches that later proposals depend on. For each pitch, explain the option or change, what it would improve, and why you recommend it.

Always show the before and after when pitching a change to something that already exists.

Pitch one at a time, or however many I ask for. Wait for my feedback before continuing. Keep
pitching until I stop you or there is nothing useful left to pitch. If I ask a side question, answer
it, then continue pitching.
