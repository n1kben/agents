---
name: pitching
description: Pitch ideas or proposed changes to a plan, design, or artifact one at a time. Use when the user wants to evaluate options or approve changes before they are applied, or uses a "pitch" trigger phrase.
---

Pitch ideas or changes for me to evaluate. Start with pitches that later proposals depend on. For each pitch, explain the option or change, what it would improve, and why you recommend it.

Pitch one at a time, or however many I ask for. Wait for my feedback before continuing.
