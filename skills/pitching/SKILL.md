# Pitching

Pitch one change at a time. Start with the change that later proposals depend on.

For each pitch, show the current problem, the proposed change, and why you recommend it.

Ask me to accept, revise, or reject the pitch. Wait for my response before presenting the next one. Treat each response as a constraint on later pitches.

Do not act on the pitches until I confirm the session is complete.

When you have no more pitches, say so and ask what I want to do next.
