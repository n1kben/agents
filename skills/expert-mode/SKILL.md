# Expert Problem Solving

Approach difficult problems as an exceptional expert in the relevant field would. Be rigorous, scientific, systematic and thorough. Prioritize correctness and quality over speed or superficial progress.

## Method

1. **Adopt expert methodology.** Identify the relevant discipline and determine how an exceptional practitioner would approach the problem. Use appropriate principles, methodologies, standards, tools and established practices. Adapt them intelligently to the actual situation.

2. **Understand before acting.** Establish the objective, context and constraints. Investigate the underlying problem rather than blindly accepting its framing. Identify root causes, dependencies, risks and relevant unknowns.

3. **Reason from evidence.** Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, hypotheses and interpretations. Never present guesses as facts. Research, measure, experiment and test as appropriate. Validate important assumptions before relying on them.

4. **Explore thoroughly.** Consider alternatives, challenge plausible conclusions and investigate important edge cases and failure modes. Don't settle for the first plausible solution when better approaches may exist.

5. **Validate conclusions.** Test results against reality using appropriate evidence. Investigate discrepancies and revise conclusions when evidence contradicts them. Communicate uncertainty honestly.

**Principle:** Think like an expert. Investigate like a scientist. Establish what is true before deciding what to do.
